DNA Tests: Genetic Analysis

DNA ancestry kits analyze your genetic material through saliva samples. These tests compare your DNA markers with large databases to estimate ethnic origins and migration patterns.

DNA tests can provide:

  • Ethnicity estimates
  • Relative matching
  • Migration history
  • Ancient population connections

However, they usually require:

  • Shipping a physical kit
  • Waiting days or weeks
  • Sharing sensitive genetic information
  • Higher costs

Is AI Ancestry Accurate?

AI ancestry analysis is different from scientific DNA testing. Instead of analyzing genes, AI identifies visual facial patterns associated with geographic regions and historical populations.

The goal is entertainment, curiosity, and heritage exploration — not medical or scientific diagnosis.
