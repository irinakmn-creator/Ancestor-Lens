Is this a DNA test?

No — Ancestor Lens is not a DNA test.

You don’t need to send saliva, wait for a lab, or share genetic data. Ancestor Lens uses advanced AI to analyze historical, cultural, and ancestral patterns based on your input. It gives you deep heritage insights instantly — directly in the app.

No labs. No shipping. No waiting. Just instant discovery.

Do I need special equipment?

No special equipment needed. All you need is your phone.

Everything works directly inside the app — no kits, no external devices, no paperwork. You answer a few guided questions, and your insights are generated instantly. It takes less than 2 minutes to begin.

Is my data secure?

Yes. Your privacy is a priority.

  • We do not collect DNA
  • We do not sell personal data
  • Your information is encrypted
  • You can delete your data anytime

Ancestor Lens is built with privacy-first architecture. Your identity stays yours.

Who is this app for?

Ancestor Lens is for:

  • Curious minds who want to explore their roots
  • People who love history & cultural identity
  • Families who enjoy discovering surprises together
  • Anyone who has ever asked: “Where do I really come from?”

It’s especially popular among Millennials & Gen Z, couples testing their “unexpected heritage”, and families reacting together (yes — grandparents love it).

If you’re curious about who you are beyond borders — this is for you.
