# Mobile usability and three new articles — 2026-10-16

## Scope and priority

1. Make store download controls easier to reach and activate on mobile, preserving existing design and navigation.
2. Publish three distinct, useful English guides informed by forum questions, with scientific/official references for factual claims.
3. Connect the guides from the homepage, blog index and related articles, and update the sitemap.
4. Improve prospective store-click reporting in the existing Clarity project without a second collector or duplicate click events.

No existing article URL was removed, merged or redirected. Existing article bodies were retained apart from three short contextual links; five intro download blocks received clearer copy and accessible headings. No ranking or conversion improvement is asserted.

- Shorter homepage subtitle retains the visual-exploration/DNA distinction and leaves the exact requested title/H1 intact.
- Hero store badges load eagerly; other imagery keeps existing responsive/lazy loading behavior.
- Store badge links have a minimum 44px target height. Compact article intro badges wrap without stretching vertically or overflowing a narrow screen.
- Five priority article intro CTAs state that the app offers visual exploration, with a free download and in-app purchases, using proper H2 headings.
- Legacy article FAQ controls now expose expanded state, control relationships and Enter/Space activation. Existing home FAQ behavior remains intact.
- Visible keyboard focus and readable illustration captions; original branding, layouts and animations retained.
- Three additional cards use the existing homepage blog-card component. Original priority cards remain present. The three new cards also appear once each at the top of the blog index.

## Clarity interpretation and measurement

The prior three-day review found six sessions flagged for dead clicks; three also had confirmed store outbound events. A Clarity flag alone therefore does not establish a broken button. Exact targets and causes were not established for all six sessions, and this update does not claim to fix all six. No individual session IDs or recordings are included in the repository.

The existing event names remain unchanged. Store activations additionally set `download_clicked=yes` and independent `download_app_store=yes` / `download_google_play=yes` flags. They support a union filter without adding per-store users together. These flags only apply after deployment. Clicks are intent signals, not verified installs. See `MEASUREMENT.md` for reporting limits and manual checks.
